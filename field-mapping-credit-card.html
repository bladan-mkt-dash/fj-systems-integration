Credit Card — Cross-System Field Map

Same business concept in three systems: two isolated payment vaults (Athena / Zenoti) that never share card data, plus a GHL multi-select under Call Outcomes → Credit Card with two system-driven options — Credit Card Athena and Credit Card Zenoti — that mirror vault presence one-way and cannot be checked manually.

Firewall (hard rule): AthenaNet Card On File and Zenoti Saved Card → Payments are separate payment instruments. Having a card in one system does not grant permission to charge, tokenize, or copy it into the other. Same plastic card may exist in both — but only via separate manual capture from the patient.

GHL path (confirmed): Folder/section Call Outcomes → field Credit Card → dropdown options Credit Card Athena and Credit Card Zenoti. Compact notation: Credit Card → Credit Card Athena / Credit Card → Credit Card Zenoti. Both options are integration-owned — staff must not check them manually; the hub sets/clears each option from the matching vault’s presence.

Integration behavior for this field family

Derive has_athena_card_on_file and has_zenoti_saved_card. Mirror into GHL field Credit Card (Call Outcomes folder): set/clear option Credit Card Athena from Athena presence; set/clear option Credit Card Zenoti from Zenoti presence. Options may be blank/checked independently. Lock or overwrite manual edits — these options are integration-owned. Never move PAN/token between vaults or into GHL.

Engineer rules of the road

Do not

Copy or reuse card tokens

No Athena → Zenoti or Zenoti → Athena payment-method write. No “use Athena card to charge Zenoti” and no reverse. PCI / product permission boundary, not just a data-quality preference.

Do

Support dual manual capture

Ops workflow assumes staff may collect the same card twice — once into each vault — when the patient needs both Athena billing and Zenoti payment. Integration must not invent a cross-vault shortcut.

Do

Mirror each vault into its GHL option

Athena present ⇔ select Credit Card Athena; Athena absent ⇔ clear it. Zenoti present ⇔ select Credit Card Zenoti; Zenoti absent ⇔ clear it. Keep writes idempotent. Do not store last4 / brand / token in GHL.

Do not

Allow manual GHL checks

Staff must not toggle these options by hand. Treat them as integration-owned (hide, lock, or overwrite on next sync). Manual edits are invalid state.

Report

Keep Admin Check List dual columns

cc_athena and cc_zenoti stay independent. Missing in one vault is still a gap even if the other vault — and its GHL option — is complete.

Truth

Independent option matrix

Valid GHL states: neither, Athena only, Zenoti only, or both. Never infer Zenoti from Athena or the reverse.

Pattern to reuse: Isolated vaults + per-source one-way presence mirrors into a multi-select field (system-driven options, no manual override). Distinct from Photo ID, which has a single Athena → GHL option and no Zenoti source.
