Credit Card — Cross-System Field Map

Canonical example for a data engineer: same business concept in three systems, two payment vaults that must never share card data, and one presence flag that should auto-update when Athena has a card on file.

Firewall (hard rule): AthenaNet Card On File and Zenoti Saved Card → Payments are separate payment instruments. Having a card in one system does not grant permission to charge, tokenize, or copy it into the other. Same plastic card may exist in both — but only via separate manual capture from the patient.

Integration behavior for this field family

Emit / store a derived boolean has_athena_card_on_file (and separately has_zenoti_saved_card for Admin Check List). On Athena card-present = true, set GHL Call Outcomes Credit Card checkbox = true. Do not implement fill-if-empty of payment tokens between Athena and Zenoti — this field is an explicit exception to the general golden-record fill-empty rule.

Engineer rules of the road

Do not

Copy or reuse card tokens

No Athena → Zenoti or Zenoti → Athena payment-method write. No “use Athena card to charge Zenoti” and no reverse. PCI / product permission boundary, not just a data-quality preference.

Do

Support dual manual capture

Ops workflow assumes staff may collect the same card twice — once into each vault — when the patient needs both Athena billing and Zenoti payment. Integration must not invent a cross-vault shortcut.

Do

Sync presence to GHL

When Athena Card On File becomes present, set GHL Call Outcomes Credit Card checkbox = checked. Prefer idempotent upsert of the boolean; do not store last4 / brand / token in GHL unless a future requirement says otherwise.

Report

Keep Admin Check List dual columns

cc_athena and cc_zenoti stay independent. Missing in one vault is still a gap even if the other vault is complete.

Pattern to reuse: For the broader field map, classify every cross-system attribute as one of Isolated vault / no sync, Manual dual entry, One-way presence/status, One-way fill-if-empty, or Two-way. Credit Card is the template for isolated vaults + one-way status.
